Frequently Asked Questions

How was this list compiled?

This list was created by combining data from box office collections, critic reviews, and audience polls across the web.

Why is my favorite movie not in the top 10?

With over 100 years of cinema, competition is tough! If you think a movie deserves a higher spot, let us know via the Feedback page.

Does this list include regional cinema (Tollywood, Kollywood, etc.)?

No, this specific list is dedicated entirely to Hindi-language cinema (Bollywood).
